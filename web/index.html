    Welcome to the paddock

    First time on track?

    The tutorial is a quiet lap of Rio de Janeiro: throttle, steering, the racing line colours, braking and how pit stops work. It takes about two minutes, then you go straight into qualifying and your first race on easy settings. You can open the tutorial again from the menu any time.
